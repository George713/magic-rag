# Magic card assistance

This project helps a player discover Magic: The Gathering cards and understand their abilities.

## Language

**Card**:
A distinct Magic: The Gathering game piece identified by its canonical characteristics and current rules text, independent of its individual releases.
_Avoid_: Printing, embedding

**Printing**:
A particular release of a card, associated with a set, language, or presentation.
_Avoid_: Card

**Card text**:
The current canonical rules text of a card, including its faces where applicable.
_Avoid_: Historical wording

**Card discovery**:
Finding cards whose characteristics or abilities match a player's description.
_Avoid_: Deck optimization

**Card explanation**:
An explanation of a card's characteristics and abilities, grounded in the available card information.
_Avoid_: Authoritative rules ruling

**Legendary card**:
A card whose type line includes the Legendary supertype.
_Avoid_: Legendary creature (when referring to legendary cards of any card type)

**Card asset**:
A visual representation of a retrieved card selected for a response, using imagery of a representative printing and its faces where applicable.
_Avoid_: Card text, printing (when referring to the visual representation itself)
